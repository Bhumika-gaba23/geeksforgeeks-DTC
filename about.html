03 / where it began

Three laptops.
One shared table.

In 2022, a handful of DTC students stayed after class to solve one more problem together. That small circle grew into workshops, hack nights, peer mentoring, and a campus-wide community of builders.

“The goal was never to create the smartest room. It was to create the room where you could ask the next question.”— the founding team
