02 / a little context

Started with
three laptops.

In 2022, a handful of DTC students started meeting after class to solve one more problem together. That tiny circle became bootcamps, hack nights, workshops and — eventually — a community of builders across campus.

“The goal was never to create the smartest room. It was to create the room where you could ask the next question.”

— the founding team
